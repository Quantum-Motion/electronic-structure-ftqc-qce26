Electronic structure calculations on a fault tolerant quantum computer

📅 Tuesday, September 15, 2026 🕐 10:00 📍 MTCC, Toronto

Overview

Attendees will learn about electronic structure calculations in chemistry, how they can be performed on a fault tolerant quantum computer, & algorithm design choices to optimise the procedure. They will gain practical experience with computational chemistry software while also exploring newly-developed quantum backends for resource calculations.

This tutorial presents joint work by Quantum Motion, Riverlane, and FACCTs (Germany).
